Japanese rules.

Write the mathematical Japanese of a textbook, in the である style throughout.
Not です・ます, and not the Japanese of a lecture.

The statement words are fixed: Theorem is 定理, Proposition is 命題, Lemma is 補題,
Corollary is 系, Definition is 定義, Remark is 注意, Example is 例, Scholium is 補注,
Exercise is 演習問題. A heading reading `Proposition 3` becomes `命題 3`, with the
number where it is.

Proper names keep their Latin spelling in running prose. Artin is Artin, Noether
is Noether, Wedderburn is Wedderburn. Katakana such as アルティン is used only
inside a term that has settled with it in the literature.
