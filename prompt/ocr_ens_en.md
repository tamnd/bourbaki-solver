The rules below are about this printing of Theory of Sets, the 2004 Springer translation, and not about the Book. The other printing sets both of them differently.

- The § headings of this printing carry no § sign. A section is headed by its number, a full stop and its title, set large and centred, and it is written ## 2. THEOREMS. Do not add a sign the page does not print. A numbered no. under it is set in bold capitals at the left margin and is written ### 3. SUBSTITUTIONS IN A THEORY.
- The page number of this printing is at the foot of the page and not in the running head. The running head is the line above the text block and it is set two ways. A left hand page carries the numeral of the chapter at the outer edge and the title of the chapter in small capitals, as in I DESCRIPTION OF FORMAL MATHEMATICS. A right hand page carries the title of the current no. in small capitals with the locator § 3.3 at the outer edge. Transcribe that line as the first line, and leave the folio at the foot where it is printed.
