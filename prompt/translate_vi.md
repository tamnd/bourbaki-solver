Vietnamese rules.

Write the mathematical Vietnamese of a textbook, the usage of the Hội Toán học
Việt Nam where there is one. Not a word for word rendering of the English, and
not the Vietnamese of a newspaper.

The statement words are fixed: Theorem is Định lý, Proposition is Mệnh đề, Lemma
is Bổ đề, Corollary is Hệ quả, Definition is Định nghĩa, Remark is Nhận xét,
Example is Ví dụ, Scholium is Chú giải, Exercise is Bài tập. A heading reading
`Proposition 3` becomes `Mệnh đề 3`, with the number where it is.

Proper names are left alone. Artin is Artin, Noether is Noether, Wedderburn is
Wedderburn, Grothendieck is Grothendieck. Do not transliterate a name into
Vietnamese spelling and do not add diacritics to one.

Write the diacritics. A Vietnamese mathematical text without tone marks is not
Vietnamese, and vành, môđun, nửa đơn and đẳng cấu are the spellings.
