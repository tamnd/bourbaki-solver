This volume is Theory of Sets and not Algebra. Everything above holds. The rules below are the notation of this Book, and they are here because a pilot of ten pages wrote each of them more than one way on one page.

- A theory is denoted by a script capital T. Write \mathscr{T}, and \mathscr{T}' and \mathscr{T}_0 for the ones that carry a mark. It is a T and never a G, and it is never \mathcal.
- The negation sign is printed as a tall corner, a horizontal bar with a stroke descending at its right end. Write \neg. Do not write \urcorner, \rceil or \mathord{\rceil}.
- Disjunction is printed as a large V between two relations. Write \vee. Do not write \bigvee, \lor or \mathop{\lor}.
- The sign that stands in an assembly where a letter was removed is a small filled square. Write \square.
- The Hilbert operator is the Greek letter tau with the letter it binds as an index: \tau_x(A). The substitution of a term for a letter is printed as (T|x)A and is transcribed exactly so, with the vertical bar.
- The labels of the criteria, the schemes and the axioms are printed as CF1 to CF8, CS1 to CS8, C1 upwards, S1 to S8 and A1 upwards. Keep them as printed, in that form, and keep the full stop the volume prints after them. A criterion is set as its label, a full stop and then the statement in italic, as in C2. *Let A be a theorem in a theory*, with no dash between the two, and it is transcribed that way.
- The § headings of this volume carry no § sign. A section is headed by its number, a full stop and its title, set large and centred, and it is written ## 2. THEOREMS. Do not add a sign the page does not print. A numbered no. under it is set in bold capitals at the left margin and is written ### 3. SUBSTITUTIONS IN A THEORY.
- The appendix of chapter I carries no number. The page prints APPENDIX alone and sets its title under it, so write ## APPENDIX and put the title on the line under it as a heading of its own.
- The page number of this volume is printed at the foot of the page and not in the running head. The running head is the line above the text block and it is set two ways. A left hand page carries the numeral of the chapter at the outer edge and the title of the chapter in small capitals, as in I DESCRIPTION OF FORMAL MATHEMATICS. A right hand page carries the title of the current no. in small capitals with the locator § 3.3 at the outer edge. Transcribe that line as the first line, and leave the folio at the foot where it is printed.
