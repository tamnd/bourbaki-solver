This Book is Theory of Sets and not Algebra. Everything above holds. The rules below are the notation of this Book, and they are here because a pilot of ten pages wrote each of them more than one way on one page. They hold of every printing of the Book, because they are about the signs the mathematics uses and not about how one publisher set the page.

- A theory is denoted by a script capital T. Write \mathscr{T}, and \mathscr{T}' and \mathscr{T}_0 for the ones that carry a mark. It is a T and never a G, and it is never \mathcal.
- The negation sign is printed as a tall corner, a horizontal bar with a stroke descending at its right end. Write \neg. Do not write \urcorner, \rceil or \mathord{\rceil}. It is not a vertical bar and it is not the digit 1: \vee A \neg B and (T|x)A are on the same page of no. 1 and the two signs in them are different signs. The page that says "the sign \neg" in words says it about the corner.
- The abbreviation of \vee \neg is printed as a double arrow with a long shaft. Write \Rightarrow. It is not \longrightarrow, \to or \Longrightarrow.
- Where the text says a letter is linked to the \tau on its left, the link is drawn on the page as a line above the assembly. It is not transcribed. Write the assembly on one line, \tau \in \square y, and let the sentence beside it say what the link is.
- Disjunction is printed as a large V between two relations. Write \vee. Do not write \bigvee, \lor or \mathop{\lor}.
- The sign that stands in an assembly where a letter was removed is a small filled square. Write \square.
- The Hilbert operator is the Greek letter tau with the letter it binds as an index: \tau_x(A). The substitution of a term for a letter is printed as (T|x)A and is transcribed exactly so, with the vertical bar.
- This Book marks a new paragraph inside a no. with a pilcrow at the head of the line, and it does it often. Write ¶ and then a space and then the paragraph, on one line. It is not the dangerous bend: the dangerous bend is a road sign drawn in the outer margin, away from the text, and the pilcrow sits at the left edge of the text block with the sentence running on from it.
- The labels of the criteria, the schemes and the axioms are printed as CF1 to CF8, CS1 to CS8, C1 upwards, S1 to S8 and A1 upwards. Keep them as printed, in that form, and keep the full stop the volume prints after them. A criterion is set as its label, a full stop and then the statement in italic, as in C2. *Let A be a theorem in a theory*, with no dash between the two, and it is transcribed that way.
- The appendix of chapter I carries no number. The page prints the word for appendix alone and sets its title under it, so write it as a level two heading and put the title on the line under it as a heading of its own.
