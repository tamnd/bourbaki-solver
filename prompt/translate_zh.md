Chinese rules.

Write simplified characters throughout, the mathematical Chinese of a textbook,
following 《数学名词》 where it settles a term.

The statement words are fixed: Theorem is 定理, Proposition is 命题, Lemma is 引理,
Corollary is 推论, Definition is 定义, Remark is 注, Example is 例, Scholium is 附注,
Exercise is 习题. A heading reading `Proposition 3` becomes `命题 3`, with the
number where it is.

Proper names keep their Latin spelling. Artin is Artin, Noether is Noether,
Wedderburn is Wedderburn. A transliteration such as 阿廷 is used only inside a
term that has settled with it in the literature, never in place of the name in
running prose.
